# 03. CROSS BENCH — Complete Services Specification

> Comprehensive functional scope, methodology, deliverables, and operational models for both Political Consulting and Public Policy Advisory.

---

## 1. Engine 1: Political & Electoral Consulting Services

### 1.1 Constituency Profiling & Voter Micro-Targeting
- **Methodology:** Multi-layer GIS demographic mapping, combining census data, historical election commission voting records (booth-by-booth), caste census estimates, and localized economic indicators.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Red/Yellow/Green booth risk classifications.
  - Micro-demographic constituency encyclopedias.
  - Swing voter propensity models.

### 1.2 24x7 Digital & Ground War Room Operations
- **Methodology:** Real-time social listening, regional TV/print monitoring, WhatsApp network tracking, and rapid response narrative deployment.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Daily intelligence briefings (7:00 AM & 9:00 PM).
  - Rapid counter-narrative multimedia packages (infographics, video explainers).
  - Opposition vulnerability dossiers.

### 1.3 Candidate Winnability & Persona Architecture
- **Methodology:** Perception gap audits, speech coaching, localized issue mapping, and track-record presentation for individual MPs, MLAs, and electoral aspirants.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Constituency connect blueprint.
  - Social media persona makeover.
  - Town hall and public engagement roadmaps.

### 1.4 Ground Cadre Mobilization & GOTV (Get Out The Vote)
- **Methodology:** Booth-level volunteer verification, booth committee deployment tracking, voter slip distribution monitoring, and polling-day turnout mechanics.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Booth commander toolkits.
  - Real-time voter turnout mobile tracking dashboard.
  - Last 72-hour voter conversion strategy.

## 3. Practice Pillar 3: Strategic PR Agency & Media Operations

### 3.1 Leadership PR & High-Authority Media Presence
- **Methodology:** Systematic personal branding and national prominence for political leaders, party spokespersons, and ministers. Placement in top-tier op-eds, podcasts, and primetime television debate coaching.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Executive spokesperson preparation and TV debate sparring sessions.
  - Placement in national and regional mainstream media outlets.
  - Personal narrative and achievements progress dossiers.

### 3.2 45-Minute Rapid Crisis PR & Counter-Disinformation
- **Methodology:** Rapid-response war room mechanism designed to neutralize political smears, media trials, leaked recordings, and viral social media rumors within a 45-minute tactical window.
- **Deliverables:**
  - 45-minute authoritative multimedia rebuttal packages.
  - Real-time fact-checking dossiers distributed to national newsdesks and ground cadres.
  - Crisis defense holding statements and legal-press coordination.

### 3.3 Institutional Press Relations & Editorial Choreography
- **Methodology:** Deep editorial relationships with national, regional, and vernacular news editors, bureau chiefs, and political anchors. Organizing synchronized press conferences.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Pan-India multi-lingual press release syndication.
  - Background press briefings and Chatham House editorial lunches.
  - Structured media monitoring dossiers and sentiment trend charts.

### 3.4 Digital PR, Influencer Command & Narrative Amplification
- **Methodology:** Multi-platform narrative dominance on X (Twitter), YouTube, Instagram, and WhatsApp broadcasting networks. Neutralizing opposition hashtags and setting public agenda.
- **Deliverables:**
  - High-impact digital campaigns and narrative video explainers.
  - Vernacular digital creator and influencer syndication.
  - Trend-monitoring telemetry and algorithm tracking dashboards.

## 2. Engine 2: Public Policy & Governance Advisory Services

### 2.1 Welfare Scheme Impact Assessment (Third-Party Audits)
- **Methodology:** Independent, empirical field surveys evaluating beneficiary satisfaction, delivery leakages, last-mile DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) efficacy, and bureaucratic bottlenecks.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Independent Scheme Health Scorecards.
  - Policy refinement memorandums.
  - Citizen sentiment analytics reports for administrative leadership.

### 2.2 Legislative Research & Policy Briefings
- **Methodology:** Objective analysis of draft bills, legislative amendments, parliamentary committee reports, and state budgetary allocations.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Executive legislative briefs (2-page actionable summaries).
  - Cross-state comparative policy benchmarking.
  - Parliamentary question and zero-hour talking points for lawmakers.

### 2.3 Citizen Sentiment & Grievance Feedback Loops
- **Methodology:** Quantitative telephonic polling, qualitative focus group discussions (FGDs), and localized administrative grievance audits.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Early-warning dashboard on public discontent.
  - Sectoral satisfaction indices (Health, Education, Infrastructure, Agriculture).

### 2.4 Strategic Advocacy & Whitepapers for NGOs & Think Tanks
- **Methodology:** Translating complex social, legal, and environmental issues into high-impact, evidence-based policy proposals for civil society organizations.
- **Deliverables:**
  - High-authority policy whitepapers.
  - Multi-stakeholder consultative roundtables.
  - Media advocacy kits for policy reform.
